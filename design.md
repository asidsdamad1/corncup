**Project:** `couple-space` · Couples' Memory & Future Planning
**Status:** source of truth for all new screens
**Runnable reference:** [`design-demo/`](design-demo/index.html) — open `design-demo/index.html` in a browser
**Superseded prompts:** [`docs/ai/design/prompt-archive.md`](docs/ai/design/prompt-archive.md)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Duyên is a **crafted keepsake journal**, not a dashboard. The governing metaphor is a scrapbook two
people keep together: sheets of warm paper laid on a soft blue desk, notes written on ruled lines,
photographs mounted on graph paper, small stamped labels pressed on at an angle.

The mood is **calm, warm, and slightly imperfect**. Nothing is machine-perfect: cards sit at half a
degree of rotation, outlines have four different corner radii, dividers are dashed rather than solid.
That deliberate imprecision is the entire point — it signals *made by hand, for one person* rather
than *generated by an app*.

Density is **airy but intimate**. Content sits in a narrow 940px column even on wide screens, because
the product is read the way a letter is read, not scanned the way a report is scanned.

**Key characteristics**

- Warm paper surfaces (`#FCFBF8`) floating on a dot-grid ground
- One consistent ink stroke (`2.2px`, `#253558`) drawing every outline
- Asymmetric "wobble" corner radii instead of uniform rounding
- Sub-degree rotation on cards, stamps, and mounted photos
- Ruled-paper backgrounds behind anything personal or hand-written in tone
- Dashed rules, dashed empty states, dashed focus rings
- Colour used for warmth and state, never for decoration

### The one hard constraint: no script typeface

This aesthetic is adapted from [`qua-sinh-nhat/index.html`](../../qua-sinh-nhat/index.html), which
leans on **Caveat** (a handwriting face) in roughly forty CSS rules — it does most of the emotional
work there. Duyên keeps **its own two typefaces** instead, so the handmade quality must be carried
by **geometry and texture, not by letterform**:

| The reference did it with Caveat | Duyên does it with |
| --- | --- |
| Handwritten headings | Plus Jakarta Sans 700, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`, `rotate(-0.6deg)` |
| Handwritten body notes | Be Vietnam Pro 400 sitting on a **ruled-paper** background (`--rule: 30px`) |
| Handwritten stamps and tags | Plus Jakarta Sans 600 uppercase `0.16em` inside a wobble-bordered chip |
| Ballpoint pen frames | `2.2px` solid ink borders + asymmetric `border-radius` |

The result reads **crafted / editorial** rather than **handwritten**. This is intentional.

> **Do not add a script, handwriting, or `cursive` font to this project.** If a screen feels too
> clean, the fix is more texture — ruled lines, dashed rules, rotation, a mounted photo — never a
> decorative typeface. Adding one would break the type system in `tailwind.config.ts` and make the
> Vietnamese diacritics noticeably worse.

## 2. Colour Palette & Roles

Every value below already exists in
[`couple-space/tailwind.config.ts`](couple-space/tailwind.config.ts) and
[`globals.css`](couple-space/src/app/globals.css). This section names them; it does not introduce
anything new.

### The four brand colours

- **Soft Cornflower Wash** (`#b2ccec`) — token `background-main`.
  The application ground, and the calm the whole product rests on. Also fills secondary chips and
  the inactive half of a toggle. Never used for text.
- **Blush Petal** (`#f5c7c7`) — token `surface-accent`.
  The warm counterweight. Carries **active state, selection, primary buttons, the filled portion of
  every progress meter**, and emotional highlights. Its scarcity is what makes it read as important —
  roughly one accent moment per screen region.
- **Whisper Paper** (`#f2f2f2`) — token `surface-text-container`.
  Text-heavy interiors: input fields, note bodies, quiet inner panels. Reduces glare when long
  Vietnamese paragraphs sit over a coloured ground.
- **Deep Ink Navy** (`#253558`) — token `ink-primary`.
  **All** typography, all icons, and every outline stroke. This is the "pen" of the system.
  **Never use pure black (`#000`).**

### Supporting values

- **Slate Blue** (`#47607c`) — token `primary`. Secondary and metadata type: timestamps, captions,
  helper text, inactive navigation labels. Deliberately never a background for body text.
- **Warm Paper** (`#FCFBF8`) — derived. The card and sheet surface. Warmer than `#f2f2f2`, which is
  why sheets feel like paper and inner panels feel like inserts.
- **Cool Paper** (`#F4F7FC`) — derived. The page ground beneath the washes, and the backing behind
  mounted photographs.
- **Mint Signal** (`#00d294`) — **completion only**. A meter reaching its goal, a finished milestone,
  the "you are here" node. Never a brand or decorative colour; if it appears, something succeeded.
- **Alert Crimson** (`#ba1a1a`) — token `error`. Validation failures only.

### Ink alphas — the quiet workhorses

Rather than introducing grays, the system dilutes the ink. This keeps every neutral in the same
colour family, which is a large part of why the palette feels coherent.

- `--ink-20` = `#25355833` — dashed rules, empty-state borders, unfilled meter tracks
- `--ink-08` = `#25355814` — ruled-paper lines, the page dot grid, soft shadows
- `--ink-05` = `#2535580d` — the faintest tint separations

### Distribution discipline

Reading down any screen you should see, in order of area:
**Cool/Warm Paper → Soft Cornflower Wash → Deep Ink Navy → Blush Petal → Whisper Paper.**
Blush Petal must stay a minority. When it starts covering large areas the interface stops feeling
like a keepsake and starts feeling like a toy.

## 3. Typography Rules

Two families, already loaded in `globals.css`. No third family, and no script face.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** — display, headings, labels, buttons, numerals.
  Geometric and slightly tight; carries authority without feeling corporate.
- **Be Vietnam Pro** — all body copy, notes, and inputs.
  Designed for Vietnamese, so diacritic stacks stay even at small sizes. This is why it must own
  every long paragraph.
- **Material Symbols Outlined** — all icons, `FILL 0` by default, `FILL 1` for the active nav item.

### The scale (already in `tailwind.config.ts` — reuse, don't reinvent)

| Token | Size / line-height | Weight | Family | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `headline-lg` | 32 / 40, `-0.02em` | 700 | Jakarta | Desktop page titles |
| `headline-lg-mobile` | 26 / 32, `-0.02em` | 700 | Jakarta | Mobile page titles |
| `headline-md` | 24 / 32 | 600 | Jakarta | Section and card titles |
| `headline-sm` | 20 / 28 | 600 | Jakarta | Sub-cards, list item titles |
| `body-lg` | 18 / 28 | 400 | Be Vietnam Pro | Lead paragraphs |
| `body-md` | 16 / 24 | 400 | Be Vietnam Pro | Default body |
| `body-sm` | 14 / 20 | 400 | Be Vietnam Pro | Captions, metadata |
| `label-md` | 14 / 16, `0.05em` | 600 | Jakarta | Buttons, form labels |
| `label-sm` | 12 / 14, `0.02em` | 500 | Jakarta | Micro-labels, nav |

### Two rules specific to this style

**Display headings rotate.** Page titles take `transform: rotate(-0.6deg)`. It is small enough to
read as intentional rather than broken, and it is what replaces the handwritten heading.

**Micro-labels are stamped, not written.** Any kicker, tag, or eyebrow label uses Jakarta 600 at
`0.68rem`, `letter-spacing: 0.16em`, uppercase, in Slate Blue. This letter-spaced-caps treatment is
the system's substitute for a handwritten annotation and appears constantly — above sections, on
timestamps, inside chips.

Body copy stays at `line-height: 1.62`, or exactly `30px` when it sits on ruled paper, so the text
lands on the lines rather than drifting between them.

## 4. Component Stylings

All of these are implemented in [`design-demo/craft.css`](design-demo/craft.css).

### Buttons

- **Shape:** small wobble radius (`--wobble-sm`), `2.2px` Deep Ink Navy border. Never a plain pill,
  never a plain rectangle.
- **Primary:** Blush Petal fill, ink border, ink label. Hover lightens to white — inverted from the
  usual "darken on hover", which keeps the page feeling like paper rather than glass.
- **Secondary:** Warm Paper fill, same ink border. Hover fills with Blush Petal.
- **Press:** `translateY(2px)`. A physical push, no ripple.
- **Icon-only:** `44px` circle, ink border — the one place a true circle is allowed.
- **Disabled:** `opacity: 0.5`, no colour change.

### Cards and sheets — two distinct surfaces

- **Sheet** (`.sheet`): Warm Paper, generously rounded `28px`, lifted shadow
  (`0 24px 44px -18px #2535582e`), **no border, no rotation**. This is the page's own paper. Use it
  for the main container of a section.
- **Card** (`.card`): Warm Paper, `2.2px` ink border, **wobble radius**, soft shadow, and
  `rotate(-0.4deg)` (or `0.5deg` for `.tilt-r`). This is something *placed on* the paper. Use it for
  discrete items: an emotion entry, a savings goal, a timeline node.

Alternating `-0.4deg` and `+0.5deg` down a list is what stops a column of cards looking mechanical.

- **Dashed card** (`.card.dashed`): `--ink-20` dashed border on Cool Paper. **Every empty state uses
  this** — an unfilled slot must look like a space waiting to be filled in, not like a broken card.

### Inputs and forms

- Whisper Paper fill, `2.2px` ink border, small wobble radius.
- Focus turns the fill white and adds the dashed focus ring. The border does **not** change colour —
  the ink stroke is constant throughout the system.
- Labels sit above the field as stamped micro-labels (§3), never as floating placeholders.
- Placeholder text is Slate Blue at 75% opacity.

### Chips and stamps

- **Chip** (`.chip`): the workhorse tag. Small wobble radius, ink border, stamped micro-label type.
  Variants: `.accent` (Blush Petal — active/primary), `.blue` (Cornflower — categorical),
  `.soft` (dashed, Slate Blue — inert metadata).
- **Stamp** (`.stamp`): a chip rotated `6–7deg` with a large numeral over a tiny caption. Reserved
  for dates, countdowns, and counts. Its job is to look pressed onto the page. Use at most one or two
  per screen — a stamp everywhere is a stamp nowhere.

### Progress meters

Never a rectangular bar. A meter is an **SVG path with a slight curve**, stroked in `--ink-20` for
the track and Blush Petal (`5.4px`, round cap) for the fill, animated by `stroke-dashoffset` from
`100` to `100 - pct` with `pathLength="100"`.

At **95% or above the fill switches to Mint Signal** — the only colour change in the component, and
the reward for nearly finishing.

### Navigation

- **Mobile (primary):** a floating dock, centred, `16px` from the bottom. Warm Paper at 95% with
  `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`, `2.4px` ink border, wobble radius. Active item takes a Blush Petal
  pill and `FILL 1` on its icon; inactive items are Slate Blue.
- The dock scrolls horizontally rather than shrinking, so all six destinations
  (`home / favorite / photo_album / lock / calendar_month / savings`) stay reachable at 390px.
- **Desktop:** the same six items sit in a fixed 256px left rail. The rail keeps its **original**
  treatment and is deliberately exempt from the ink-outline rule: no background of its own, so the
  Soft Cornflower ground reads straight through it, with a `border-r border-white/40` hairline, a
  `rounded-r-[2.5rem]` corner and a whisper shadow. Only the items inside it are craft-styled — the
  active one takes a Blush Petal wobble chip with an ink border.
  **Do not give the rail a paper background**; the sidebar and the ground are one surface here.

  > Historical note: the rail's original class was `bg-[rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)]`, which never
  > compiled because of the stray spaces inside the brackets — so it has always rendered fully
  > transparent. That rendered appearance is what is preserved. If a genuine 20% white glass panel
  > is ever wanted, the working equivalent is `bg-white/20`.

### Timeline

- A **dashed vertical spine** (`--ink-20`, `7px` on / `7px` off) drawn as a background gradient.
- **Nodes** are `15px` ink-outlined circles with a `4px` Cool Paper halo, so they punch through the
  spine cleanly. Fill states: Warm Paper (upcoming), Blush Petal (done), Mint Signal (current).
- Each node's content is a `.card`; the expanded node reveals a `.sheet` beneath it.

### Photo galleries — justified rows, never a cropped square

Albums use `.jr`, which lays photos out in **justified rows**: every row is flush to both margins,
every photo in a row shares one height, and **no photo is ever cropped**. A uniform square grid is
forbidden here — it beheads portraits and shaves the sky off landscapes, which is the opposite of
what a keepsake album is for.

The mechanism is pure CSS, no measuring and no JavaScript:

| Property | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flex-basis` | `ar × --jr-h` | a first guess at the photo's width |
| `flex-grow` | `ar` | leftover space is shared **in proportion to width** |
| `aspect-ratio` (frame) | `ar` | height follows from width, so nothing is cropped |

Because final width is `ar × (H + leftover ÷ Σar)`, dividing by `ar` gives the same number for every
item in the row — a flush row of equal-height photos, solved by the flex engine.

Three rules govern it:

1. **`--ar` comes from stored `width`/`height`, never from measuring the image.** `MemoryPhoto`
   records intrinsic pixel size at upload. That is what lets the row maths run on the server, and it
   reserves the correct box before the bytes arrive so the page never jumps.
2. **The last row must not stretch.** `.jr::after` is a zero-width item with `flex-grow: 999` that
   soaks up the slack, leaving the final row at target height and left-aligned. Without it a lone
   trailing photo inflates to the full width.
3. **A photo with no stored size still lays out.** `aspectOf()` falls back to 3:2 rather than
   collapsing the row.

Photos keep the `.mat` graph-paper mount and alternating sub-degree rotation. The lightbox mounts
the print the other way round — **the mat hugs the photo**, rather than the photo filling a fixed
mat, or a portrait shot sits in a landscape box with the mount's gradient showing as bars beside it.

### Map

`.map-sheet` is a paper panel holding a **hand-drawn** map of Vietnam: an ink coastline under a
`feTurbulence` displacement filter so the line reads as pen, not vector. It is deliberately a
sketch, not a survey map, and the UI says so.

- **Pins** are Blush Petal circles on a `2.2px` ink stroke, with a 21px invisible halo so a fingertip
  needn't be precise. Hollow dashed pins mark places not yet visited.
- A **dashed thread** joins trips in date order.
- Pins are keyboard-reachable (`role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, Enter/Space) and carry the standard
  dashed ink focus ring.
- Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa appear in an inset box, per the usual convention on Vietnamese maps.

**Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa are drawn where they are**, not in the usual corner inset. That is why the
viewBox is 800 × 1040 (lng 102→118°E, lat 5.93→23.5°N) and the mainland only occupies the left half
— the East Sea really is that wide. The inset box is the conventional shortcut, but it hides the one
fact the real placement makes obvious: from the central coast out to Hoàng Sa (~320 km) is about
two thirds of the run from the south-central coast out to Trường Sa (~500 km).

Administratively they are districts, not provinces, so they are **not** rows in `PROVINCES` —
adding them would quietly turn 63 into 65. They live in `ARCHIPELAGOS` and are reached through
`ALIAS`: huyện đảo Hoàng Sa → Đà Nẵng, huyện Trường Sa → Khánh Hoà. The map itself is labelled
just **Hoàng Sa** and **Trường Sa**; the dashed ring already says it is a group, and the
administrative line was more text than a sketch this size can carry.

**Pins are small, but their tap target is not.** The drawn pin is a `7.5` body under a `15` halo;
above both sits an unpainted `22` circle (`.pin-hit`) that does nothing but catch the pointer. That
is what lets the art shrink to suit the map without the target shrinking with it — about 51px across
on a 460px-wide map, comfortably over the usual 44px minimum. It is filled `transparent`, never
`none`: a transparent *colour* still hit-tests, `none` does not.

Map type is sized independently of pin geometry: `.pin-tag` (`8.5px`) for place names, `20` units
for the two archipelagos. Both counter-scale with zoom, so they hold their size on screen — zooming
separates crowded pins rather than enlarging their labels.

The map **zooms and pans**: wheel or the `+` / `−` buttons, drag to move, pinch on touch, and a
reset. Two rules make it behave:

- **Pins counter-scale.** Each pin carries `scale(1 / zoom)`, so it stays the same size on screen at
  any zoom. Without it, a pin at 6× would be the size of a province.
- **The page keeps its scroll until you zoom in.** `touch-action` is `pan-y` at full extent and
  `none` once zoomed, so a map in the middle of a long page never traps a thumb trying to scroll past.

> **Places are derived, never stored.** `resolveProvince()` turns a memory's own `location` string
> into a province; coordinates come from `PROVINCES` in `src/lib/provinces.ts`. Nobody maintains
> coordinates per memory. A location the resolver cannot place is **shown to the user**, never
> silently dropped — an undercount is worse than an honest question.

**Adding a province is one row** in `PROVINCES` — name, `lat`, `lng`, `region`. All 63 of the
pre-2025 arrangement are already there, so in practice you only add *places*: a town that is not its
provincial capital needs a row in `PLACE_COORD` (so the pin lands on Mộc Châu, not Sơn La city) and
one in `ALIAS` (so the text "Mộc Châu" resolves to Sơn La).

The coastline anchors are offset slightly seaward of the landmark cities they were derived from.
Drawn exactly on those coordinates, capitals like Quy Nhơn, Nha Trang and Phan Thiết sat *on* the
boundary — half in the water, and ambiguous to the point-in-polygon check that guards this.

### Locked note panel

The one component allowed extra texture, because concealment is its entire function: layered radial
gradients over a Blush Petal base, producing a mottled "scratch card" surface. Over it sit a
wobble-bordered lock tile rotated `-4deg`, four wide passcode fields, and a hint line.

A wrong code shakes the panel `±7px` over `0.4s` and turns the fields Alert Crimson. A correct code
hides the cover and plays the standard reveal (§6).

> **Security note.** The passcode check in `design-demo/secrets.html` is client-side and exists only
> to demonstrate the interaction. The real gate must live in a Server Action; note content must never
> reach the browser before the server has authorised it.

## 5. Layout Principles

- **Mobile-first, and mobile-real.** Couples use this on phones. Every layout is designed at 390px
  first, then allowed to widen. Touch targets are `44px` minimum.
- **940px maximum content width**, centred, with `18px` side padding. Wide screens get more margin,
  not more columns — the reading column stays letter-width.
- **`124px` bottom padding on `<body>`** so the floating dock never covers the last element.
- **Spacing** uses the existing tokens: `stack-lg` (2rem) between sections, `stack-md` (1rem)
  within a card, `stack-sm` (0.5rem) between tightly related lines.
- **Section rhythm:** every section opens with a `.sec-head` — a rotated outlined numeral
  (Blush Petal fill, `1.3px` ink stroke, `-6deg`), the title, and a one-line subtitle. The numerals
  give a long scroll the feel of pages in a journal.
- **Grids** are `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...))`, so cards reflow rather than squeeze. Minimums:
  `210px` for emotion cards, `230px` for note cards, `250px` for savings cards, `140px` for photos.
- **No horizontal page scroll, ever.** Wide content (a photo strip, the dock) scrolls inside its own
  container.

## 6. Motion & Interaction

Motion is **paper-like**: things rise, settle, and get placed. Nothing bounces, spins, or slides in
from off-screen.

| Purpose | Duration & easing |
| --- | --- |
| Reveal (locked note, expanding node) | `0.5s` opacity + `0.55s cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1)` on translate/scale, from `translateY(24px) scale(0.97)` |
| Hover / state change | `0.18s ease` |
| Button press | `0.14s ease`, `translateY(2px)` |
| Meter draw-on | `0.9s cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1)` on `stroke-dashoffset` |
| Error shake | `0.4s cubic-bezier(0.3, 1.2, 0.4, 1)`, `±7px` |

`transform-origin` for reveals is `50% 0` — content unfolds downward from where it was, like a page
being opened.

### Focus is part of the visual language

Every interactive element takes the **same dashed ink focus ring**:
`outline: 3px dashed #253558; outline-offset: 3px`. No browser-default glow anywhere. The dashed
outline matches the dashed rules and dashed empty states, so keyboard navigation looks designed
rather than tolerated.

### Reduced motion is mandatory

Every page ships this block. The reference implementation has it and the current React app does not —
this is a gap to close during migration:

```css
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  *, *::before, *::after {
    animation-duration: .001s !important;
    animation-iteration-count: 1 !important;
    transition-duration: .001s !important;
  }
  html { scroll-behavior: auto; }
}
```

## 7. Signature Recipes

Five moves produce most of the style. Copy them verbatim.

**1 · The wobble border** — the single most identifying detail.

```css
--wobble:    255px 16px 226px 18px / 18px 224px 16px 252px;  /* cards, panels */
--wobble-sm: 130px  8px 116px 10px / 10px 114px  9px 128px;  /* chips, buttons, fields */

.card {
  border: 2.2px solid #253558;
  border-radius: var(--wobble);
  transform: rotate(-0.4deg);
}
```

**2 · The ground: Soft Cornflower, plus two layers of desk**

The colour is non-negotiable — `#b2ccec` stays the base, and it is the only thing painted on
`body` itself, so the app still reads as blue. Everything else lives in two fixed, non-interactive
pseudo-element layers behind the content:

| Layer | z-index | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `body::after` | `-2` | Four soft radial washes (blush, white, Primary Fixed) that **drift very slowly** |
| `body::before` | `-1` | The desk: graph-paper rules, ink dots at the crossings, three ballpoint doodles, paper grain, vignette. **Static.** |

```css
body { background-color: var(--color-background-main); }   /* #b2ccec, unchanged */

body::after {                     /* drifting light */
  position: fixed; inset: -25% auto auto -25%;
  width: 150%; height: 150%;      /* oversized so the drift never exposes an edge */
  z-index: -2; pointer-events: none;
  background-image:
    radial-gradient(38% 34% at 84% 12%, #f5c7c759, transparent 70%),
    radial-gradient(42% 38% at  8% 34%, #ffffff4d, transparent 72%),
    radial-gradient(40% 36% at 96% 82%, #f5c7c74d, transparent 68%),
    radial-gradient(46% 40% at 26% 96%, #d0e4ff66, transparent 70%);
  animation: ground-drift 54s ease-in-out infinite alternate;
}

@keyframes ground-drift {
  from { transform: translate3d(-1.6%, -1.2%, 0) scale(1); }
  to   { transform: translate3d( 1.6%,  1.4%, 0) scale(1.04); }
}
```

Three rules govern this, and they are the difference between atmosphere and mush:

1. **Move it with `transform`, never `background-position`.** Position animation forces a full
   repaint of a viewport-sized layer every frame; a transform on an oversized element is
   composited on the GPU. The layer is 150% so the drift never exposes an edge.
2. **The texture does not move.** Grain, rules and dots that drift stop reading as paper and start
   reading as noise. Only the light moves, and at 54s per pass it should never catch the eye.
3. **`prefers-reduced-motion` parks it.** The drift is switched off explicitly — the blanket
   `animation-duration: .001s` rule would otherwise snap it to its end keyframe.

Doodles are hidden below 640px, where they crowd the content; the paper texture stays.

**3 · Ruled note paper** — what makes text read as hand-written without a script font.

```css
.ruled {
  background-image: repeating-linear-gradient(to bottom,
    transparent 0 29px, #25355814 29px 30px);
  border-left: 2px solid #f5c7c7cc;
  padding: 6px 0 2px 16px;
}
.ruled p { margin: 0; line-height: 30px; }   /* must equal the stripe pitch */
.ruled p + p { margin-top: 30px; }
```

**4 · The dashed rule** — replaces every solid divider.

```css
.rule {
  height: 1px; border: 0; margin: 14px 0;
  background: repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, #25355833 0 6px, transparent 6px 12px);
}
```

**5 · The photo mount** — graph-paper backing so images look pasted in.

```css
.mat {
  padding: 10px;
  background: #f4f7fc;
  background-image:
    linear-gradient(#25355812 1px, transparent 1px),
    linear-gradient(90deg, #25355812 1px, transparent 1px);
  background-size: 15px 15px;
  box-shadow: 0 15px 30px -10px #25355814;
  transform: rotate(-1.4deg);
}
```

## 8. Implementation Status

The style is applied across `couple-space/src`. This section records where it lives and the
structural facts a future contributor needs.

### Tailwind v4: `tailwind.config.ts` is NOT loaded

The app uses `@import "tailwindcss"` with **no `@config` directive**, so `tailwind.config.ts`
never reaches the build. It is left in place untouched, but it is dead weight.

That mattered. The type scale and shadows existed *only* in that file, so roughly **284 uses of
`text-headline-*` / `text-body-*` / `text-label-*`, plus 10 of `shadow-soft` / `shadow-card`, were
silently producing no font-size, weight, or shadow at all** — the app rendered at browser-default
16px throughout. Those tokens now live in the `@theme` block of
[`globals.css`](couple-space/src/app/globals.css), which is the single source of truth.
**Add new tokens there, not to `tailwind.config.ts`.**

### Where the style lives

- [`globals.css`](couple-space/src/app/globals.css) — `@theme` tokens (colours, spacing, type
  scale, shadows, `--radius-wobble` and `--radius-wobble-sm`), the paper `body` ground, the global
  dashed focus ring, the `prefers-reduced-motion` block, and every primitive in
  `@layer components`: `sheet`, `card` (+ `-tilt-r` `-flat` `-accent` `-wash` `-ink` `-dashed`),
  `panel`, `rule`, `rule-v`, `ruled`, `chip` (+ `-accent` `-blue` `-soft`), `stamp`,
  `btn` (+ `-accent` `-ink` `-sm` `-icon`), `field` (+ `-label` `-error`), `tabs`/`tab`/`tab-on`,
  `mat`/`mat-inner`, `timeline`/`node-dot`, `dock`, `overlay`,
  `modal` (+ `-head` `-body` `-foot`), `toast` (+ `-ok` `-warn` `-error`),
  `masonry-grid`/`masonry-item`, `reveal`/`reveal-in`, `shake`.
  These mirror [`design-demo/craft.css`](design-demo/craft.css) so the doc and the app cannot drift.
- [`ui/Card.tsx`](couple-space/src/components/ui/Card.tsx) — exports **`Sheet`** (page paper: no
  border, no rotation) and **`Card`** (ink border, wobble radius, rotation) with `tilt` and

- **Modals, popups and notifications** all sit on the shared `overlay` + `modal` primitives, so

### Layout preserved, with three defects fixed

Grid spans, sidebar offsets and page structure were kept as they were. Three genuine bugs were
corrected on the way through:

1. `SecretBoxManagement` offset its content by `md:ml-64` while the sidebar only appears at `lg`,
   pushing content 256px right with no sidebar present between 768–1024px. Now `lg:ml-64`.
2. `SecretBoxLocked`, `SecretBoxUnlocked` and `DateReviewEmotions` each hardcoded their own
   `aside`/`nav` sidebar *and* rendered `NavBar`, producing two sidebars. They now use the shared
   `NavBar` only.
3. Off-palette colours (`amber-*`, `emerald-*`, `red-500`) and one native `alert()` were replaced
   with the brand `toast` variants.

### Known gaps

- **`PROVINCE_TARGET` is 34, and needs confirming.** Vietnam reorganised its provincial units
  around July 2025, from 63 down to 34. The constant in `src/lib/provinces.ts` reflects that, but
  the merge map is deliberately *not* encoded — it must come from the official text, not from
  memory. Until someone checks it, treat the denominator as provisional.
- **`KNOWN_PROVINCES` and `ALIAS` are seed data, not a complete gazetteer.** They cover the places
  in `mockData.ts` plus the suggested destinations. Every new province the couple visits needs a
  row in `PROVINCE_COORD` and `REGION_OF`, or its pin will not appear. The unresolved-locations
  panel on the memories screen is what makes that omission visible.
- **Photos are still held as blob URLs in React state.** `CreateMemoryModal` captures real pixel
  dimensions and preserves selection order, but nothing is uploaded or persisted; a refresh loses
  everything. Google Photos was evaluated and ruled out as a *hosting* source — a Picker API
  `baseUrl` requires an `Authorization` header that `<img>` cannot send, and expires in ~60
  minutes. Any future import path must download the bytes once and re-host them.
- **Shared state is in memory only.** `src/lib/memoryStore.ts` holds memories and the travel
  wishlist behind `useSyncExternalStore`, so the detail and overview screens agree with each other
  when something is marked featured or a photo is added. A refresh still resets to the seed data.
- The passcode check in `SecretBoxPasswordPopup` is client-side and cosmetic. The real gate must
  be a Server Action; note content must never reach the browser before the server has authorised it.
- Buttons that do nothing remain on the screens outside this one — `WeeklyDatePlanner` (9),
  `EmotionPulse` (5), `PlansAndSavings` (6), `HomePage` (5), `SecretBox*` (5), `CreateSecretBox` (3).
  The memories screen has none.

### "Featured" is marked, never inferred

`Memory.featured` is set by hand — the star on the detail hero, or the badge on the card. It is
deliberately **not** derived from `date` or `rating`:

- Deriving it from date meant "Kỷ niệm nổi bật" silently held whatever was newest. A Saturday
  coffee outranked a week in Hà Giang because it happened later.
- Deriving it from `rating` would have the app score the relationship on the couple's behalf.

`rating` and `tags` are shown but never acted on: stars on the album card and the detail sheet,
tags as dashed chips. Both were stored on every memory and displayed nowhere before.

## 9. Checklist for a New Screen

- [ ] Ground is the paper background; the reading column caps at 940px
- [ ] Opens with a `.sec-head` — rotated numeral, title, one-line subtitle
- [ ] Sheets hold sections; cards hold items, with alternating rotation
- [ ] Blush Petal appears once or twice, never as a large field
- [ ] All type is Plus Jakarta Sans or Be Vietnam Pro — **no script font**

- [ ] Personal or note-like copy sits on ruled paper at `30px` line-height
- [ ] Dividers are dashed; empty states are dashed cards with a clear action
- [ ] Progress is a curved SVG meter, mint at ≥95%
- [ ] Every interactive element shows the dashed ink focus ring
- [ ] Works at 390px with no horizontal scroll; dock does not cover content
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured
